Discover African Wine in Los Angeles

Discover African wines, pairings, and wine flights at Flavors From Afar, a Pan-African bistro and wine bar on Hollywood Boulevard.

Wine flight

Four 2 oz pours: So Far Out Sparkling, Ferme Blanc, Le Gris Rosé and Ferme Rouge.

Taste profileA tour from bright and bubbly to crisp white, dry rosé and a smooth, spiced red.

The story

Can't decide? Our flight pours a taste of every wine we serve by the glass, from California bubbles to a Moroccan red, so you can find your favorite before choosing a glass or a bottle.

Discover Wine Wednesday

White

Ndubs Chenin Blanc

Bottle

A dry South African Chenin Blanc from Western Cape vineyards, made with native yeast and minimal sulfur.

Taste profileOrchard fruit, quince and grapefruit zest over a firm, mouthwatering acidity that cuts through chile and coconut alike.

The story

Ndubs is the easygoing range from Aslina Wines, the label of Ntsiki Biyela. She grew up in rural KwaZulu-Natal and won a scholarship to study winemaking at Stellenbosch University. She became South Africa's first Black woman winemaker and named Aslina after her late grandmother.

Made in South Africa

Kumusha Sauvignon Blanc

Bottle

A South African Sauvignon Blanc from Slanghoek in the Breedekloof Valley, made with minimal intervention and natural fermentation.

Taste profileZippy and fresh, with melon, passion fruit and guava, bright acidity and a clean finish.

The story

Kumusha means “your home, your origin, your roots” in Shona, the language of Zimbabwe. Founder Tinashe Nyamudoka moved from Harare to Cape Town in 2008. He rose from polishing cutlery in a restaurant to award-winning sommelier before starting his own label.

Made in Slanghoek, South Africa

Terre Blanche

Bottle

A Moroccan white blend of Viognier and Chardonnay from organically farmed vineyards cooled by Atlantic winds.

Taste profileStone fruit and white flowers from the Viognier, body from the Chardonnay, and the fresh, mineral, slightly salty edge the estate is known for.

The story

Terre Blanche is one of the two cornerstone wines of La Ferme Rouge, an estate in Morocco's Zaër region about 45 km from the Atlantic. The estate was laid out in 1908 and made its first vintage in 1933. Bordeaux winemaker Jacques Poulain helped relaunch it in 2009.

La Ferme Rouge · Made in Zaër region, Morocco

Ferme Blanc

Glass or bottle

A Moroccan white blend of Sauvignon Blanc and Vermentino, poured by the glass and the bottle.

Taste profileFresh, light and easy-drinking, with crisp acidity and a clean finish.

The story

Ferme Blanc comes from Morocco's Zaër region, where steady breezes off the Atlantic temper the heat. It blends Sauvignon Blanc with Vermentino, a Mediterranean grape at home in warm coastal vineyards.

Made in Zaër region, Morocco

Suggested with Coconut Curry, Piri Piri Prawns

Sparkling

So Far Out "Flood Light Snow"

Glass or bottle

A dry, lightly cloudy, pale-pink pét-nat with soft, natural bubbles.

Taste profileDry, bright and refreshing, with strawberry, tangerine and citrus peel and a faintly cidery finish.

The story

This pét-nat is made the ancestral way, the oldest method for making sparkling wine. It is bottled while still fermenting, so the bubbles come from the wine itself. The grapes grow in Los Alamos, in California's Santa Barbara County.

Made in Santa Barbara, California

Suggested with Catch of the Day

Ikenga Palm Wine (500 ml)

Bottle

Nigerian-inspired sparkling palm wine · Soursop, nectar, lightly tropical, refreshing, and gently sweet. One of its kind.

Taste profileGently sweet and creamy, with soft bubbles, soursop and nectar notes and light acidity.

The story

Palm wine is one of West Africa's oldest drinks and a centerpiece of Nigerian celebrations; at Igbo weddings, the bride carries a cup of it through the crowd to find her groom. Ikenga's Nigerian winemaker, scientist and artist Onye Ahanotu, crafts it in small batches in Berkeley, California, as an homage to fresh morning palm wine.

Made in Berkeley, California

Rosé

Le Gris

Glass or bottle

A pale, dry Moroccan rosé of Cinsault, aged in stainless steel.

Taste profilePale, dry and nervy, with red berry, strawberry and citrus pith, crisp minerality and real grip. It handles spice better than almost anything on our list.

The story

Le Gris is the rosé of La Ferme Rouge, made from Cinsault, one of the estate's old-vine grapes. The vineyards are farmed organically and the grapes are picked by hand.

La Ferme Rouge · Made in Morocco

Suggested with Half Chicken, Goat Pepper Soup

Red

Ndubs Pinotage

Bottle

A South African Pinotage from Western Cape vineyards, made with native yeast and minimal sulfur.

Taste profileBlack plum, ripe cherry and a dusting of black pepper. Medium-bodied and smooth, with a dry finish built for braises and the grill.

The story

Pinotage is South Africa's own grape, created at Stellenbosch University in 1925 by crossing Pinot Noir and Cinsault. This one comes from the Ndubs range of Ntsiki Biyela's Aslina Wines.

Made in South Africa

Kumusha Cabernet Sauvignon

Bottle

A South African Cabernet Sauvignon from Slanghoek, made with minimal intervention and natural fermentation.

Taste profileRed berry, plum and spice, made for juicy red meat.

The story

Kumusha wines are made with winemaker Atti Louw at Opstal Estate in the Breedekloof Valley. Tinashe Nyamudoka started the label in part to bring more Black ownership and representation into South African wine.

Made in South Africa

Terre Rouge

Bottle

A Moroccan red blend of 80% Syrah and 20% Tempranillo.

Taste profileBlack pepper, olive and dried herb, the same aromatic family as ras el hanout, with dark fruit underneath.

The story

Terre Rouge is the red half of La Ferme Rouge's cornerstone pair, the wines the estate says represent its origins. It comes from vineyards near Morocco's Atlantic coast, where steady ocean winds temper the heat.

La Ferme Rouge · Made in Zaër region, Morocco

Ferme Rouge

Glass or bottle

A Moroccan red blend of 80% Cabernet Sauvignon, 10% Carignan and 10% Marselan, poured by the glass and the bottle.

Taste profileApproachable and smooth, with ripe fruit, spice and a soft finish.

The story

Ferme Rouge comes from Morocco's Zaër region. It leads with Cabernet Sauvignon, rounded out with Carignan, one of Morocco's old-vine grapes, and Marselan.

Made in Morocco

Suggested with Potjie Oxtail, Mechoui Lamb Shank, Koshari

Honey wine

Saba Tej

Glass or bottle

Ethiopian-style honey wine made with orange-blossom honey, served chilled.

Taste profileSemi-sweet and citrus-lifted, smoother than the Axum, with floral honey and a gentle herbal finish from the gesho.

The story

Tej is Ethiopia's ancient honey wine, poured at weddings, holidays and in traditional tej houses. It is flavored with gesho, a native shrub often called Ethiopian hops, and is traditionally sipped from a round, long-necked flask called a berele. Saba Tej comes from Saba Tej Co., an American maker of Ethiopian-style tej.

Saba Tej Co. · Made in United States

Axum Tej

Glass or bottle

Ethiopian-style wildflower-honey wine, served chilled.

Taste profileOff-dry, bold and floral, with an earthy undertone, a crisp hoppy edge and a tannic, herbal finish.

The story

Axum Tej is named for Aksum, the ancient Ethiopian kingdom and holy city. It is made for Saba Tej Co. in the traditional style, with gesho balancing the honey's sweetness.

Saba Tej Co. · Made in United States

Beer

Casa

A crisp, clean Moroccan lager from Casablanca.

Taste profileLight, crisp and clean, with soft malt sweetness and a refreshing finish.

The story

Casa is billed as “the original beer from Casablanca,” brewed and bottled in Morocco by Brasseries du Maroc. Beer came to Morocco with the French in the 20th century, and a cold lager is now a classic partner for grilled meats and spice.

Made in Morocco

Non-alcoholic

West African Bissap

Cup or large carafe

A chilled hibiscus drink, a West African favorite, by the cup or the large carafe.

Taste profileTart, floral and refreshing, with deep, berry-like notes similar to cranberry.

The story

Bissap is steeped from dried hibiscus flowers, and every West African family has its own recipe, often brightened with ginger, mint or fruit. Sold by street vendors from Dakar to Lagos, it is the cold drink of celebrations and hot afternoons.

North African Lemonade

Cup or large carafe

Chilled lemonade made in the North African style, by the cup or the large carafe.

Taste profileBright, tart and refreshing, with a clean citrus finish.

The story

Across North Africa, fresh lemonade is the go-to refresher on hot afternoons, often brightened with fresh mint or a splash of orange blossom water.

Ethiopian Coffee

Coffee brewed from Ethiopian beans.

Taste profileBright and aromatic, with the floral and fruity notes Ethiopian coffees are known for and a smooth finish.

The story

Ethiopia is the birthplace of coffee. Legend says a goatherd named Kaldi noticed his goats dancing after eating the red cherries of a wild shrub. In Ethiopian homes, coffee is still roasted, brewed and shared in a ceremony that can last for hours.

Somali Shaah

Somali spiced black tea.

Taste profileWarm and aromatic, with fragrant spices over a bold black tea.

The story

Shaah, Somali for tea, is at the center of Somali hospitality, poured for guests and shared every afternoon. It is traditionally brewed with warm spices such as cardamom, cinnamon and cloves, a legacy of Somalia's place on the Indian Ocean spice routes.

Kenyan Tea

Kenyan black tea.

Taste profileBold, brisk and full-bodied, with a bright, malty finish.

The story

Kenya is one of the world's leading tea producers, and its highland gardens grow bold, bright black tea. At home, Kenyans drink chai, tea boiled together with milk, morning and afternoon.

Moroccan Mint Tea

Green tea with fresh mint.

Taste profileFresh and cooling, with bright mint over gentle green tea.

The story

In Morocco, mint tea is the gesture of welcome: green tea brewed with fresh spearmint, poured from high above the glass to raise a light foam. Guests are traditionally offered three glasses.
