Frequently asked questions

What is Friends From Afar?

Friends From Afar is a Pan-African Friendsgiving celebration at Flavors From Afar, created for people spending Thanksgiving away from family or simply looking for a welcoming holiday gathering in Los Angeles.

When is Friends From Afar?

Wednesday, November 25, 2026, Thanksgiving Eve.

What time does the event start?

Doors open at 5 PM. There are two dinner seatings: 5:30 PM and 8 PM.

How much does it cost?

Tickets are $75 per person. Early Bird tickets are $65 through November 15. Early Bird pricing ended November 15.

What's included with my ticket?

A non-alcoholic welcome beverage, three-course Pan-African dinner, live Afrobeats DJ, communal or private table seating, and a Thanksgiving Day to-go meal.

What comes in the Thanksgiving day to-go plate?

Turkey, Thanksgiving sides, and dessert.

What are the dinner entrée options?

Guests may choose turkey, beef, fish, or vegetarian.

Can I attend by myself?

Absolutely. Friends From Afar welcomes solo guests. Our communal Friendsgiving table is designed to help people meet, connect, and celebrate together.

Can I come with friends?

Yes. You can reserve together and request private table seating, subject to availability.

Is there a communal table?

Yes. Guests may choose to join the communal Friendsgiving table.

Are alcoholic drinks included?

No. A non-alcoholic welcome beverage is included. African wine is available by the glass for $10, and wine and beer are sold separately.

Do I need a ticket?

Yes. Advance tickets are required through the OpenTable Experience. Walk-ins are not accepted.

How many seats are available?

There are 40 seats at each of the two seatings, for a total of 80 seats.

What is give a seat?

Give a Seat is a $75 sponsorship that provides a Friends From Afar experience for a refugee or immigrant spending their first Thanksgiving in Los Angeles.

Can I sponsor more than one guest?

Yes. You can sponsor multiple seats, subject to the availability of the sponsorship program.

Will I hear about the seat I sponsored?

We'll share an update about the impact of your contribution while respecting the sponsored guest's privacy.

Where is Friends From Afar held?

Flavors From Afar, 5652 Hollywood Blvd, Los Angeles, CA 90028.
