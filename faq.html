Frequently asked questions

Good to Know Before You Go

Get answers about dining at Flavors From Afar in East Hollywood, including reservations, groups, dietary questions, gift cards and arrival details.

Everything you need to know before joining us at Flavors From Afar. Have another question? Call us at (213) 720-5588.

Reservations & dining

Do I need a reservation?

Reservations are recommended, especially for dinner, weekends, and special experiences. Use the Reservations button on our website to check availability through OpenTable.

Do you accept walk-ins?

Walk-in seating may be available depending on current reservations and dining room capacity. We recommend making a reservation to secure your preferred dining time.

How do I modify or cancel my reservation?

You can modify or cancel your reservation through your OpenTable confirmation. For additional assistance, please call us at (213) 720-5588. Any applicable reservation terms will be shown when booking.

Can you accommodate large parties?

Yes, we welcome group dinners, celebrations, and special gatherings. For larger parties or customized arrangements, please visit our Private Events page to submit an inquiry.

Families, pets & dress code

Are children welcome?

Families and children of all ages are always welcome at Flavors from Afar! We have two high chairs available on a first-come, first-served basis. Whether you're joining us for lunch, an early dinner, or an evening out, we look forward to welcoming the whole family!

Are pets allowed?

Pet dogs are welcome on our outdoor patio only. Pets are not permitted inside the restaurant, with no exceptions.

Dogs must remain under their owner's control and off restaurant furniture.

Is there a dress code?

Yes. We maintain a dress code to preserve a comfortable dining experience for all guests.

Full clothing and footwear are required. Shirtless or topless attire, sandals, and bare feet are not permitted.

We appreciate your understanding and cooperation.

Food, wine & dietary needs

What type of cuisine does Flavors From Afar serve?

Flavors From Afar is a Pan-African Bistro & Wine Bar featuring dishes inspired by the ingredients, traditions, and culinary cultures of North, West, East, and Southern Africa.

Our menu pairs distinctive flavors with contemporary presentations, offering a chance to explore African cuisine in Los Angeles.

Is your food halal?

Our menu is halal, and we also serve alcoholic beverages.

Do you offer vegetarian and vegan options?

Yes. Our menu includes vegetarian and vegan selections.

Availability may change, so please review our current menu and inform your server of any dietary preferences.

Can you accommodate food allergies?

Please inform our team of any food allergies or dietary restrictions when making your reservation and again before ordering.

While we take care in preparing our dishes, cross-contact may occur, and we cannot guarantee an entirely allergen-free environment.

Do you serve alcoholic and non-alcoholic beverages?

Yes. Our beverage program features a selection of African wines and non-alcoholic options.

Explore our Wine & Drinks page to discover the current selection.

Can I bring my own wine?

Yes. Our corkage fee is $45 per bottle, with a maximum of one outside bottle per party.

Experiences, celebrations & events

Do you host weekly dining experiences?

Yes! Flavors From Afar offers rotating dining experiences designed around food, wine, music, and culture.

Our programming includes Wine Wednesday, Passport Hour, and additional brunch, cultural dining, and live-music experiences as announced.

Visit our Experiences page for current programming, schedules, and reservations.

Do special events require advance tickets?

Some experiences are available through regular reservations, while others require advance ticket purchases.

Each event page provides the date, time, price, inclusions, availability, and applicable booking terms.

We recommend booking early for special events with limited seating.

Can I celebrate my birthday at Flavors From Afar?

Absolutely! We welcome birthday dinners and group celebrations.

Visit our Birthday Celebration or Private Events page to learn about available packages and arrangements.

Do you offer private events and catering?

Yes. We welcome inquiries for private events, corporate gatherings, celebrations, and catering.

Visit our Private Events or Catering pages to share your event details, including the preferred date, guest count, and service format.

Our team will help you explore the available options.
